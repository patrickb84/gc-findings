## What was done, in order

1. **Saturday morning** (7 talks after the pilot), then **Saturday afternoon** (10 talks). Each talk was read by an Opus subagent working from the rubric, applied with `apply-read.mjs`, and checked by `pnpm check`.
2. **Scripture passages.** `groupPassages` groups citations that share a verse. A citation spanning several chapters (3 Nephi 11–28) stays separate from the verses inside it. Notion gained a **Scriptures** database.
3. **Sunday morning and afternoon** (15 talks), which finished the conference.
4. **Publishing** every batch to Notion: talk rows with their per-talk Highlights view, Highlights rows, Index and Scriptures syncs, the What I found page, and the root intro.
5. **Big picture readings:** four sections of interpretation, added at Patrick's request and kept separate from the counted patterns (below).
6. **GitHub push.**

## Parser fixes made along the way

Each was written test-first (`tests/scriptures.test.ts`, `tests/parse-talk.test.ts`).

- Ranges written with a figure dash (U+2012) were read as NaN. They now parse like a hyphen or an en dash.
- A footnote reference with no book ("see also verse 12") takes its book from the previous reference and its chapter from the link. Elder Hathaway's "verse 12" is Alma 7:12.
- "Psalm 55:22" is read as the book of Psalms.
- Footnote link text is entity-decoded, so `D&amp;C` becomes `D&C`, and "D&C" is an alias for the Doctrine and Covenants.
- Spoken forms now parse: "41st chapter of Isaiah," (trailing punctuation ignored), "Isaiah chapter 58", and any article of faith by its ordinal ("thirteenth article of faith").
- The Light family excludes "burden is light" and "light thing", which are false hits. The exclude list lives in the data, so other families can use it.

## What the conference says: the four Big picture readings

These open the What I found page. They are **Claude's reading of the whole**, built on the counted patterns and marked `"kind": "reading"` in `data/discoveries.json`. Every quote in them was checked word for word against the highlights.

1. **Comfort is the centre of the conference.** The passage cited most is Mosiah 18:8–11, "mourn with those that mourn", in six talks. Next is Alma 7:11–12, the Savior's succor, in five. Grief stories run through every session. Read together, the conference's doctrine is that the Savior carries grief with us and asks us to carry it with each other.
2. **Being seen.** The Good Samaritan appears in four talks and the pool of Bethesda in three. Sister Chibota builds her talk on *sawubona* ("I see you"). Elder Sinclair tells of the woman at the well, and three talks return to being a child of God. President Uchtdorf observes that the sacrament is "the only ordinance we receive during which we are not addressed by name".
3. **The advice: turn to Christ, then to the person in front of you.** About 63 of the 184 invitations, in 19 talks, ask for something done for another person, more than any other kind. Turning to Christ is the most widespread ask. The means are small and daily.
4. **The promise: strength for the load, and a love that cannot be lost.** Hardly anyone promises that a trial will end. Strength is the most common promise. Sixteen promises in eight talks say that what God gives cannot be taken away. Promises of outward blessings are fewer than ten of the 196.

The counts in readings 3 and 4 come from tagging the invitations and promises by keyword, then checking the tags by hand, so they are approximate.

Other discoveries that stood out among the 28 patterns:

- Elder Gong says "temple" 54 times, more than the other 36 talks combined. The conference ends with the Salt Lake Temple opening to the public on April 5, 2027.
- Two Eyrings, a day apart, reach for the same two passages.
- President Oaks is cited 46 times in 23 talks and President Nelson 29 times in 16. The hand-off between prophets shows in the footnotes.
- Phones appear in five talks, as both gift and thief.
- Elder Kyungu gives fourteen phrases to stop using and twenty to start.

### Rules for publishing

- Never send the **My notes** property. It belongs to Patrick.
- Never change **Status** on an approved or rejected Index row.
- Never delete a Notion row that disappears from the JSON. List it on the To do page instead.
- Everything that needs Patrick goes on the **To do** page as a checkbox. Claude never ticks one.
- Commits carry no AI attribution.

### What worked for publishing

- **Subagents:** Opus subagents with exact-copy payloads, about 300 rows each, up to 50 rows per `create-pages` call. Each wrote a `Highlight ID → url` map to a file. Every batch came back complete with no duplicates.
- **SQL query quota:** it ran out mid-session. These work without it:
  - **Rows mode** with a filter, such as `Highlight ID` or `Passage` with `string_is`, or an OR group of values (18 at once worked). Up to 100 rows come back per call.
  - **View mode**, which allows at most 1000 mention targets, so the Talks view must be read with `page_size` 12. Use it to check Highlights counts per talk against the JSON.
- **Scriptures sync:** new rows get a fractional Order between their neighbours in canon order. A row whose page id was never saved is found by its current Passage label.
- **Small page changes:** a targeted `update_content` edit is cheaper and safer than replacing the whole page.
